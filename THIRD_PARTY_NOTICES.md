The iSelfie SDK for Flutter is a plugin over the native iSelfie SDKs for iOS (`ISelfieSDK`) and
Android (`ai.iselfie:iselfie-sdk`). The third-party components inside those native SDKs, and their
licenses, are listed in
[ISelfieSDK's THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES](https://github.com/AiZtech-Labs/ISelfieSDK/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
